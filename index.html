Vishnu's Cosmic Ocean — Asian Art Museum

Opens Thu Oct 24

The largest ancient bronze ever found in Southeast Asia — a monumental Reclining Vishnu buried for centuries and rediscovered in 1936 — makes its final international tour stop right here in SF. This is a genuine once-in-a-lifetime cultural moment; the piece is unlikely to travel again after this. On view through January 25, 2027.

CulturalMuseumArtCivic CenterOpening Week
