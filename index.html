More this week

Brand New at Bill Graham Civic

Wed Nov 11

If you know, you know. Brand New is one of the most beloved and historically elusive bands in emo and post-hardcore, and any appearance is a genuine event. Bill Graham Civic Auditorium is the perfect room for their dense, layered guitar work and heavy introspective energy. Do not sleep on this one.

EmoPost-HardcoreOne Night OnlyLive MusicTBD

The Damned - Final Damnation 50 Tour

Wed Nov 11 · 8pm

One of the original UK punk bands is celebrating 50 years and calling it a farewell tour, which makes this a certified bucket-list moment for punk and goth fans. The Fillmore is an iconic room for a show this historically significant. If you have ever even casually liked punk music, go.

PunkGothFarewell TourThe FillmoreTBD

Olafur Arnalds at the Castro Theater

Nov 11-12

The Icelandic sound artist who blends neo-classical piano and strings with lush electronic textures is doing two nights at the Castro, which is honestly the most perfectly matched venue-artist pairing imaginable. His live show is a full sensory experience that feels more like stepping into a film than attending a concert. A genuinely special way to spend an evening.

Neo-ClassicalElectronicAtmosphericCastro TheaterTBD

Dirtybird Night Market at Thrive City

Fri Nov 6 · 5-10pm

Free DJ sets, vendors, games, and cocktails right outside Chase Center on a Friday evening. This is the perfect low-commitment start to the weekend where you can grab a drink, dance a little, shop a little, and see where the night takes you. Bring friends or go solo and make some.

FreeOutdoorsDJNight MarketFree

SYML at the Castro Theatre

Fri Nov 6

SYML performing his Solo in North America tour inside the ornate Castro Theatre is one of those rare venue-and-artist combinations that feels almost too perfect. His cinematic, emotionally atmospheric indie folk hits differently in a room with that much history and character. A quietly stunning Friday night option.

Indie FolkAtmosphericOne Night OnlyCastro TheaterTBD

SF Fall Cider Fest

Sat Nov 7 · 12-4pm

A whole festival dedicated entirely to cider at SPARK Social SF in Mission Bay, which is exactly the kind of very good idea that belongs in November. The outdoor setting is lively, the vibes are autumnal, and you will almost certainly discover a new favorite. 21+ and very much worth the afternoon.

CiderOutdoors21+Mission BayTicketed

Foy Vance at Great American Music Hall

Sun Nov 8 · 7pm (Doors 6pm)

The Irish singer-songwriter with one of the most deeply soulful voices in folk and blues is playing one of SF's most beautiful historic rooms, joined by Bonnie Bishop and Ryan McMullan. This triple bill is a rare treat and the ornate GAMH interior makes everything feel more special. A perfect Sunday evening.

FolkSoulGAMHSundayTBD

Fort Mason Night Market

Fri Nov 6 · 5-10pm

Food, local vendors, and a Friday evening by the waterfront at Fort Mason. Free to attend and the views alone are worth showing up for. A lovely and very San Francisco way to ease into the weekend before things get louder elsewhere.

FreeOutdoorsNight MarketFort MasonFree

Russian Circles w/ Primitive Man at GAMH

Thu Nov 12 · 8pm (Doors 7pm)

The Chicago post-metal trio who do crushing, all-instrumental heaviness are playing the Great American Music Hall with doom-metal bruisers Primitive Man opening. This is one of the most intense and well-curated double bills of the whole week. Tickets are $30 advance and absolutely worth every dollar.

Post-MetalDoomInstrumentalGAMH$30 ADV

Exploratorium After Dark: Unplug and Play

Thu Nov 12 · 6-10pm

The Exploratorium opens its doors after hours for adults only and this month the theme is unplugging and playing, which sounds like a genuinely fun and different way to spend a Thursday. 18+ only, and it hits differently when the kids are not around. Great solo or group option.

Adults OnlyInteractivePier 15ThursdayPaid

de Young Museum - Free Admission Weekend

Sat-Sat Nov 7-8

Bank of America Museums on Us means Bay Area residents get into the de Young for free this Saturday and Sunday. The permanent collection alone is worth the trip, and Golden Gate Park in November has a whole moody, beautiful thing going on. A completely free and genuinely lovely way to spend a weekend morning.

FreeMuseumGolden Gate ParkWeekendFree

ACT - John Proctor Is the Villain (Opens!)

Thu Nov 12

Kimberly Belflower's Tony-nominated play gets its West Coast premiere at ACT's Toni Rembe Theater, directed by Jess McLeod in collaboration with Seattle Rep and Center Theatre Group. It follows a high school class reading The Crucible and asking very sharp questions about who the story is really about. Opening night energy is always electric.
