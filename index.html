Movie Under the Stars

Blankets, The Princess Diaries, and dumplings at midnight.

  • 5:30 PM
    Grab provisions
    Pick up snacks and a bottle of something at a corner store near Union Square. Or pack from home if you're organized like that.
  • 6:00 PM
    Claim your spot at Union Square
    The free outdoor movie series has pre-show activities starting at 6. Lay out a blanket, settle in while the sky turns pink.
  • Sunset
    The Princess Diaries rolls
    A perfect crowd movie. Fun even if you've seen it. Especially if you've seen it.
  • ~10 PM
    Late-night dumplings in Chinatown
    It's a 10-minute walk. Good Fortune Dumpling House or wherever looks good and open. End the night with soup dumplings and no bill over $25.
The Midrange Move ~$80–120 for two

Mission Cocktail Crawl

New bars, good food, and a neighborhood that rewards wandering.

  • 6:30 PM
    Start at Stray Dog
    New cafe-bar on Utah & 24th. Pandan lattes during the day, pan-Asian cocktails at night. Shanghai-inspired, tiny, and just opened. Get an early drink while it's still mellow.
  • 7:30 PM
    Dinner nearby
    Walk to your pick. Jilli for Korean drinking food and makgeolli (the popcorn fried chicken is mandatory). Or Big Finish for wine under $14/glass and cacio e pepe.
  • 9:30 PM
    Nightcap at the Madrone parklet
    Madrone Art Bar on Haight is doing a book sale in their parklet Saturday, but any night the bar itself is a vibe — murals, DJs, and a crowd that's been coming for years.
The Splurge ~$150–200 for two

Symphony & Speakeasy

St. Vincent with an orchestra, then cocktails in a hidden lounge. Thursday only.

  • 6:00 PM
    Early dinner at Hayes Valley
    Davies Symphony Hall is right by Hayes Valley. Grab dinner before the show — lots of walkable options. Keep it light, you want to be energized not sleepy.
  • 7:30 PM
    St. Vincent w/ the SF Symphony
    Annie Clark's catalog — "Los Ageless," "Cruel," "Digital Witness" — performed with a full orchestra conducted by Jules Buckley. First-ever orchestral run. It's going to be extraordinary.
  • ~10 PM
    Cocktails at Valley Club
    Cab to Hotel G's mezzanine in Union Square. Low lights, velvet corners, and The Lovemaker — a Negroni riff with raspberry and cocoa butter. The kind of place where you stay longer than planned.
