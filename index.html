RM x SFMOMA: Between You and Me

Ongoing · Museum Hours

BTS's RM has co-curated an exhibition with SFMOMA bringing together 200 works from both of their collections — it's genuinely fascinating and drawing a wonderfully unexpected crowd of art lovers and stans alike. First-floor installations are free to see, and the full show opened this October.

Asian Art Museum – Free First Sunday (Nov 1)

Sun Nov 1 · Museum Hours

November 1st is Free First Sunday at the Asian Art Museum, where you can catch Ha Chong-Hyun's sweeping retrospective of six decades of innovative abstraction AND a once-in-a-lifetime visit from Southeast Asia's largest ancient bronze of Vishnu. Completely free, genuinely world-class. Go before brunch.
