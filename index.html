Editor's Picks

Violent Femmes at Stern Grove

Sun Aug 2 · 2pm

Free concert in the eucalyptus-lined natural amphitheater — it's the 89th season of Stern Grove and the Femmes are exactly the kind of band that makes this series feel like magic. Capacity is capped at 10,000 so you need a free reservation. Doors at noon. Bring a blanket, bring layers, don't be late.

Free Music

Jerry Day — 24th Anniversary

Sat Aug 1 · 11:30am–5:45pm

Free all-day festival at Jerry Garcia Amphitheater in McLaren Park. Melvin Seals & JGB, Stu Allen and The Mars Hotel, The Jazz Mafia, and more. After the main show wraps, Jerry Night sends the party into the Excelsior neighborhood bars. The whole thing is a love letter to SF — go soak it in.

Free Music Festival

52nd Annual Nihonmachi Street Fair

Sat–Sun Aug 1–2 · 11am–6pm

Since 1974, Japantown's Peace Plaza has turned into the best block party of the summer for this one weekend. AAPI food vendors, artisan stalls, live music on two stages, and the kind of community energy you can't manufacture. Completely free, family-friendly, and genuinely one of SF's great institutions.

Free Festival

Andrew Bird with the SF Symphony

Sat Aug 1 · 7:30pm · Davies Hall

The violinist, songwriter, and world-class whistler joins the full SF Symphony for what promises to be a genuinely special night. Bird's looping, genre-jumping style was practically built for an orchestral setting — this combination shouldn't work as well as it does, and that's exactly why it does. Don't sleep on it.

Classical / Indie $$$ · From ~$183

Treasures of the Pharaohs Opens at de Young

Opens Fri Aug 1 · Through Jan 2027

Artifacts from the tomb of Yuya and Thuya in the Valley of the Kings make their U.S. debut at the de Young this Friday. It's going to be packed all fall — going opening weekend is actually the smart call for once. Pair it with a walk through the park and you've got a full afternoon.

Exhibition New Opening $$ · Museum + exhibit ticket

Dark Star Orchestra + Melvin Seals & JGB · Greek Theatre

Fri Jul 31–Sat Aug 1 · Berkeley

Two nights of Grateful Dead music at the legendary outdoor amphitheater across the bay. Dark Star does full show recreations down to the set lists; Melvin Seals played keys with Jerry himself for 20 years. Go Friday night, then recover at Jerry Day in McLaren Park on Saturday. That's a weekend.

Grateful Dead $$ · Check SeatGeek

Old 97's · The Fillmore

Wed Aug 5 · 8pm

The beloved alt-country Texan rockers return to the Fillmore. Twangy, hook-laden, energetic — the kind of show where everyone ends up knowing more words than they thought they did. Doors at 7. Come early, grab your spot at the rail.

Alt-Country $$ · From ~$62

Yerba Buena Gardens Festival: ChoreoFest

Sat Aug 1 · 2–4pm · Free

Free contemporary dance showcase on the Great Lawn — choreographers and performers take over the outdoor esplanade for two hours. RSVP ahead to guarantee entry. Pre-show poetry workshop at noon with Nia Pearl if you want to make a full afternoon of it.

Free Dance

Yerba Buena Center for the Arts' current exhibition — presented in collaboration with the GLBT Historical Society — runs through August 23. Wednesday night brings a free public program tied to the work. YBCA is genuinely one of the best art spaces in the city and it doesn't get enough love.

Free classic arcade games, cheap drinks, DJ nights on weekends. Divisadero back room is massive and weirdly great.

Outdoor Yoga at Dolores Park

Free community yoga Saturdays at 10am on the south slope. Bring your own mat. All levels. Show up, breathe, enjoy the view.

Oasis Drag Shows

SF's premier drag venue in SoMa. Brunches, late nights, themed revues all week. Check their calendar for this week's lineup.

City Lights Bookstore Events

Free readings, book launches, and poetry nights in North Beach almost every week. The upstairs poetry room alone is worth the trip — check their calendar.

Alemany Flea Market

Every Sunday 7am–3pm. SF's oldest flea market — junk, treasures, produce, characters. Free to browse. Cash only. Get there early for the good stuff.
