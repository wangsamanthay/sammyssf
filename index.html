More this week

Lightscape at SF Botanical Garden - Opening Night

Fri Nov 20 · 5:00 PM

One million lights transform Golden Gate Park into a glowing winter wonderland tonight - and every night through January. Walk a one-mile trail through luminous art installations, optical illusions, and all-new color and sound experiences woven through the garden's ancient trees. Grab tickets fast because weekend slots sell out early.

OutdoorsOpening WeekDate NightOpens Tonight

Great Dickens Christmas Fair - Opening Weekend

Sat Nov 21 + Sun Nov 22 · All Day

Victorian London takes over the Cow Palace this weekend as the 42nd Annual Great Dickens Christmas Fair swings open its doors. Hundreds of costumed characters roam 140,000 square feet of pubs, music halls, festive shops, and theatrical streets straight out of a Dickens novel. Active military get in free the first two weekends, and early bird tickets save you a few bucks.

Sat Nov 21 · 8:00 PM

John Darnielle and company wrap up their extraordinary six-night Fillmore residency tonight with 'Closers,' the grand finale of a week-long journey through their catalog. This is the kind of run that becomes a legend in SF concert history - intimate, emotional, and utterly one-of-a-kind. If you only catch one night of this residency, make it the last one.

The Mountain Goats - Night Five: Cast of Thousands

Fri Nov 20 · 8:00 PM

Night Five of the Mountain Goats' epic Fillmore residency digs into 'Cast of Thousands,' one of the band's most beloved eras. The Fillmore's intimate size makes every set here feel like a secret show just for you, and John Darnielle's storytelling between songs is as good as the music itself. Friday night at the Fillmore - does it get more SF than that?

SF SPCA Holiday Windows at Macy's - Opens Today

Fri Nov 20 · Noon

For 40 years running, the SF SPCA has filled Macy's Union Square windows with the most irresistible holiday display in the city - adorable, adoptable puppies and kittens doing their cutest thing for passersby on Powell Street. It is completely free, it is open Wednesday through Sunday through New Year's Eve, and yes, you are absolutely allowed to press your nose against the glass. Fair warning: you may leave with a new pet.

Divine Offering: Celebrating the Legacy of Alice Coltrane

Sun Nov 22 · 8:00 PM

Sita Michelle Coltrane leads a rare and reverential tribute to her mother, Alice Coltrane, one of jazz's most transcendent spiritual artists. Presented by GAMH and Psyched! Fest, this is exactly the kind of soul-nourishing, once-in-a-blue-moon event that makes San Francisco's music scene so special. Tickets run $39.50 to $45 and this one will sell out.

SFMOMA - Sarah Sze: Forever is Composed of Nows - Opening

Opens Sat Nov 21 · Free Floor 1

Sarah Sze takes over SFMOMA's massive ground-floor atrium for her most ambitious commission yet - three paintings up to 27 feet tall colliding with video projection, sound, and light that spill up through multiple floors of the museum. It is free to experience on the first floor, which is a genuinely wild gift. This one is going to become the must-see installation of the season.

Dear San Francisco: Home for the Holidays at Club Fugazi

Nightly · 7:30 PM

The critically adored circus-meets-theater experience at Club Fugazi gets a full holiday makeover for its festive seasonal run. World-class acrobats from the 7 Fingers collective perform aerial acts, Korean plank, Chinese pole, juggling, and hand-to-trap inside the intimate North Beach venue. It is the most charming date night in the city right now, full stop.

The Nightmare Before Christmas with Live Orchestra

Tue Nov 24 · 7:30 PM

Tim Burton's beloved dark fantasy gets the full Davies Symphony Hall treatment - the film plays on screen while a live orchestra performs Danny Elfman's iconic score in real time. This is an annual SF tradition that hits a little different when you are watching Jack and Sally while a world-class ensemble plays five feet away. Perfect mid-week Thanksgiving treat.

Exploratorium After Dark: Glow Opening

Fri Nov 20 · 6:00 PM

Exploratorium's beloved Glow exhibition kicks off its season with an adults-only After Dark opening night at Pier 15 - luminescent creatures, glowing T. rex skeletons, optical illusions, and bioluminescent wonders await. Weekend daytime Glow events also run Saturday and Sunday for all ages. It is science, it is art, and it is an absolute blast.

SF Turkey Trot in Golden Gate Park

Thu Nov 26 · 8:00 AM

Start Thanksgiving morning the right way with the 24th annual Turkey Trot through Golden Gate Park - choose from a 5-mile run, a 3-mile run, or a 100-meter dash for the little ones. It is festive, it is free-spirited, and burning those pre-feast calories with a few hundred happy strangers is genuinely one of the best feelings this city has to offer. Register online in advance.

ActiveAnnual TraditionFamilyThanksgiving Morning

Indigenous Peoples Thanksgiving Sunrise Ceremony - Alcatraz

Thu Nov 26 · 4:15 AM

Watch the sun rise over the Bay from Alcatraz Island at this powerful and moving annual gathering organized by the International Indian Treaty Council, commemorating the 1969-71 occupation of Alcatraz by the Indians of All Tribes. The boat ticket runs $12 for adults and the ceremony itself is free. It is a profound and unforgettable way to spend Thanksgiving morning.
