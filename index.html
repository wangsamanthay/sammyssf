Beetlejuice & Waterfront Bites

Spooky season, bay views, and a night that costs basically nothing.

  • 4:30 PM
    Swing by Fort Mason Night Market first
    It's free and it's on the way. Browse the West Coast Craft vendors, grab something to eat from the food stalls, and soak up the waterfront golden hour before heading south to Dogpatch.
  • 5:00 PM
    Settle in at Crane Cove Park
    Crane Cove is a beautiful little park in Dogpatch right on the water. Bring a blanket, find your spot on the grass, and watch the sky go dark over the bay while the pre-show atmosphere builds.
  • Sunset
    Beetlejuice rolls
    It's October. It's outdoors. It's Beetlejuice. There is genuinely no better combination. Bring a jacket — it gets cold in Dogpatch once the sun drops.
  • ~8:30 PM
    Nightcap in the neighborhood
    Dogpatch has a few good spots to land after. Standard Deviant at Pier 70 is a 10-minute walk — huge industrial taproom, waterfront, solid beers. End the night on a high note without spending much.
The Midrange Move ~$90–130 for two

Market Street Arts Night + Dinner

A walkable art spectacle, then something good to eat nearby.

  • 5:30 PM
    Early dinner in Hayes Valley or Civic Center
    Get dinner before the festival crowds build. Hayes Valley has excellent options within walking distance of Market — grab a table at Monsieur Benjamin or any of the neighborhood spots you've been meaning to try.
  • 7:00 PM
    Hit the Market Street Arts & Theater Festival
    Six blocks of programming: aerialists, drag karaoke on a cable car, pop-up theater, a free main stage. It's inaugural, it's free, and it runs until 10pm. Walk the whole stretch — some of the best stuff will be tucked into side doors and plazas.
  • 9:30 PM
    Cocktails at Valley Club
    Hotel G's mezzanine lounge in Union Square is a 10-minute walk from the festival's eastern end. Low lights, velvet corners, inventive cocktails. The kind of bar you stay in longer than you planned. Perfect way to close an unexpectedly great Saturday.
The Splurge ~$200–280 for two

Ghibli Night at Davies Symphony Hall

Joe Hisaishi conducting his own music. Thursday only. Do not miss this.

  • 5:30 PM
    Dinner at Shinka in FiDi
    Brand new restaurant inside The Jay Hotel — a French-Japanese fusion tasting menu from a Jean-Georges alum. Opening this week and already generating serious buzz. Pre-theater dinner feels very right for tonight's itinerary.
  • 7:00 PM
    Settle into Davies Symphony Hall
    Get there with time to wander the lobby, grab a drink, find your seats. The hall itself is gorgeous. Tonight Joe Hisaishi — the composer behind Spirited Away, Princess Mononoke, Howl's Moving Castle — is conducting his own work. This is the definition of a bucket-list concert.
  • 7:30 PM
    Joe Hisaishi: Hisaishi Conducts Hisaishi
    Sit back. Let it wash over you. You're going to hear music you've loved your whole life performed live by the person who wrote it, with a full orchestra. There's nothing else to say.
  • ~10 PM
    Bar Shoji for a nightcap
    Head to SoMa's Bar Shoji — wagyu-fat-washed whisky, a fondue burger, and a dark moody room that feels just right after a transcendent concert. You'll want somewhere to sit and talk about what you just heard.
