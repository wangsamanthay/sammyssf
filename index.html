Low-Key Ways to Meet People

Running Clubs

November Project (free, Wed + Fri 6:30am, various locations), SF Road Runners, Midnight Runners (Thursday nights). Show up, run, make friends. No sign-up needed for most.

Book Clubs & Reading Groups

Green Apple Books and City Lights both host regular reading groups. Also check Meetup for genre-specific clubs — romance, sci-fi, nonfiction all have active SF groups.

Volunteer Groups

One Brick (drop-in volunteering, no commitment), SF Parks Alliance, Glide Memorial. Great way to meet people while doing something good.

Classes & Workshops

Pottery at Claymakers, cooking at 18 Reasons, dance at ODC. Sign up solo — the class format makes conversation happen naturally.

ZogSports Leagues

More competitive than Volo/Recess. Bowling, kickball, flag football, softball. Post-game bar sponsorships mean every game night ends at a bar together.
