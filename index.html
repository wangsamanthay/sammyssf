Low-Key Ways to Meet People

Running Clubs

November Project (free, Wed + Fri 6:30am). Midnight Runners (Thu nights). Show up, run, make friends.

SalsaCrazy Saturdays

Salsa and bachata lessons + open dancing. No partner needed. Great low-pressure way to meet people through movement.

Book Clubs

Green Apple Books and City Lights host regular reading groups. Check Meetup for genre-specific clubs.

Volunteer

One Brick (drop-in, no commitment), SF Parks Alliance, Glide Memorial. Meet people while doing something good.

Classes

Pottery at Claymakers, cooking at 18 Reasons, dance at ODC. Sign up solo — the format makes conversation natural.
